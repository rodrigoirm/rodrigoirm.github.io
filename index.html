About me

Hello and welcome to my personal website! I earned my BSc in Physics from University of São Paulo in 2023 and completed an MSc in Physics at the University of Campinas in 2026, with a thesis entitled "Information protocols and nonlocality in probabilistic theories", under the supervision of Prof. Marcelo Terra Cunha.

Research

My research interests revolve around generalized probabilistic theories, Bell nonlocality, higher-order quantum theory, combinatorial optimization and complexity theory.
