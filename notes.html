Theses

Master's thesis

My master's thesis provides a gentle introduction to Bell nonlocality and the framework of generalized probabilistic theories. In the thesis, I explored the similarities and differences between quantum theory and other non-classical theories. In particular, I proved that, under a composition rule that enlarges the set of entangled states, Tsirelson's bound for a specific multipartite Bell inequality is still satisfied.

Tensor product of vector spaces

At some point during my undergraduate years, tensors started popping up everywhere and they were usually presented as mysterious objects with tons of indices and that are characterized by the way they transform under changes of coordinate systems. Personally, I do not like this presentation: first, it does not define what kind of mathematical object a tensor is; second, it prevents students from getting a fairly simple exposition to the mathematical notion of taking a quotient.

In these notes, I try to carefully construct the tensor product of vector spaces and characterize it by its universal property.
